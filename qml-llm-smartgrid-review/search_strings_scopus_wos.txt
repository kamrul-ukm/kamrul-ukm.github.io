NOTES
============================================================
- The plain word "transformer" is deliberately left out of the LLM block,
  because it pulls in thousands of power-transformer papers.
- Small language models (SLMs) and small-large model collaboration terms are
  included in the language-model block. "SLM" can also mean spatial light
  modulator or selective laser melting; such records are removed at screening.
- No publisher filter is applied at search time. The publisher restriction
  (IEEE, Elsevier, Springer, ACM, Wiley/IET, MDPI) is applied during screening
  from the exported Publisher field, so no records are lost to name variants.
- For the PRISMA log, record: date of search, total hits per database, and the
  number exported.
